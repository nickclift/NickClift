# What Claude Can Do on Your Phone

Claude is available on mobile through the **Claude app** (iOS and Android) and via your mobile browser. Here's what you can do:

## Conversations
- Chat with Claude in natural language
- Ask questions, get explanations, and have extended conversations
- Continue conversations across sessions

## Writing & Editing
- Draft emails, messages, essays, and documents
- Proofread and improve your writing
- Summarize long text

## Analysis & Research
- Analyze photos and images you take or share
- Answer questions about what's in a picture
- Research topics and get detailed explanations

## Coding
- Write, review, and debug code
- Explain code snippets
- Help with programming concepts

## Creative Tasks
- Brainstorm ideas
- Write stories, poems, or scripts
- Generate creative content

## Productivity
- Create lists and plans
- Help organize your thoughts
- Draft or respond to messages

## Voice Input
- Use your phone's built-in voice-to-text to speak your messages to Claude

## Limitations on Mobile
- File system access is limited to what your terminal environment allows
- No direct integration with phone apps (calls, contacts, etc.)

## Getting Started
- **iOS**: Download from the App Store — search "Claude"
- **Android**: Download from the Google Play Store — search "Claude"
- **Browser**: Visit claude.ai from any mobile browser
